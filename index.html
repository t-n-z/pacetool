Pace

Type digits only: 3 then 5 then 2 gives 3:52. Seconds past 59 carry into minutes. The cross clears it.
Falling tone when slower than target minus tolerance. Rising tone only when faster than target plus threshold. Silent in between. The tolerances move the tones only: the clock still starts at target pace and time on pace still counts only at or above it.
Same digit entry: 1, 2, 0, 0 gives 12:00. Left blank there is no countdown. The countdown starts when the clock does, at target pace, and ends the run when it runs out.
Tap Start, keep the screen on and the phone in hand or on an arm. The clock starts by itself when you reach target pace and stops after about 100 m at half speed.
